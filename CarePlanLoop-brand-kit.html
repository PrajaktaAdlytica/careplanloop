One route, one checkpoint.

The open loop is a work route that stays traceable and stops for a person. The warm point stays visible in light and dark contexts.

Clear space: at least one checkpoint width. The route gap must remain open. Keep the supplied colors and proportions; use the standalone mark only where the wordmark cannot fit.

Designed for work that pauses.

Illustration and motion reveal what happened, where the evidence came from, and who must look next.

01 / Source

Keep evidence attached

Fine lines connect an event to its source and timestamp. Reported information remains visibly un-interpreted.

02 / Ownership

Name the next reviewer

Draw the route to a role and a dated action. Use text and icons alongside color to explain status.

03 / Pause

Stop at human review

The path ends at the reviewer checkpoint. No motion implies a diagnosis, recommendation, or approval.
