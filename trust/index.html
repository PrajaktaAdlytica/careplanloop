03Prototype data note

Inquiry behavior

The current form does not collect or send submissions.

The pilot inquiry is a front-end interaction only. It does not transmit or store information, and it warns visitors not to enter patient details or health information.

Before enabling a real form or analytics, confirm the actual data flow, privacy notice/version, consent timestamp, deletion-request route, retention, and receiving system.
