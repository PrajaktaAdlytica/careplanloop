PROVENANCEWhat arrived, from where?

OWNERSHIPWho reviews next?

BOUNDARYWhat remains separate?

Consent, report, staff action, clinician sign-off, and billing evidence are distinct.

The product concept includes no diagnosis, treatment recommendation, dose change, patient outreach, independent clinical triage, or automatic billing determination. Permissions, evidence rules, and escalation criteria must be agreed with a pilot sponsor before a real workflow is considered.
