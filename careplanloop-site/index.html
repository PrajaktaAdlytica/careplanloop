02THE GAP

The work is distributed

Enrollment, plans, reports, and follow-up live in different places.

The hypothesis: care teams need a clearer view of what happened, who owns the next step, and what remains unresolved.

Teams already use nurses, EHR care plans, outsourced CCM services, and RPM platforms. Those tools address parts of the workflow; the handoff between them is what we want to understand.

    06PROPOSED MODULES

    Five separate workflow tests

    Support the handoff at each stage.

    These are proposed modules, not live product plans. The initial pilot slice is a one-condition care-team task/consent timeline and RPM exception queue.

    01

    Enrollment/consent and eligibility review queue

    Bring enrollment and consent status into a human review queue; do not automate eligibility decisions.

    Proposed module
    02

    Clinician-approved personalized care-plan task tracker

    Keep clinician-approved tasks visible with a named owner and dated follow-up.

    Proposed module
    03

    RPM exception triage routed to clinicians

    Preserve source and context, then route an exception to a human reviewer. No independent clinical triage.

    First-slice focus
    04

    Contact/time documentation and reconciliation for billing staff

    Keep staff activity evidence distinct from device reports and billing determinations.

    Proposed module
    05

    Longitudinal gap and escalation analytics across care teams

    Explore follow-up gaps and escalation visibility after workflow access and measurement are established.

    07WORKFLOW FIT

    Work around the team's current system

    Orchestration is the hypothesis—not another sensor.

    CarePlanLoop would need to fit beside the people and tools already in the loop.

    Nurses, EHR care plans, outsourced CCM vendors, and RPM platforms address adjacent pieces of this work. Switching costs, device-feed rights, clinical staffing, and authorized data access still need to be tested.
