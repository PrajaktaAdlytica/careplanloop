01Workflow hypothesis

Problem to examine

A clearer record of where work waits.

Tasks can lose their owner between care-plan review, patient follow-up, and team handoff. A proposed tracker would expose owner, due date, linked source, review state, and unresolved items.

02Boundary

What this concept does not do

Human judgment stays with the authorized owner.

No automatic care-plan generation, diagnosis, treatment recommendation, medication or dose change, or external patient message.

Concept screen only. No live integration, patient data, clinical advice, or billing assurance is represented.
