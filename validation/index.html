01Primary metric

Predefined measurement

Follow-up documentation by the assigned owner within local SLA.

Primary measure: percentage of required care-team follow-ups documented by the assigned owner within the locally defined SLA, compared with a stated baseline and denominator.

BASELINEDefine before replay
