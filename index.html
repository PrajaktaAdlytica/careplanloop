10Clear boundaries

What this concept does—and does not do

The handoff is operational. Clinical judgment stays human.

CCM and RPM are distinct services. A device reading, automated touch, staff action, clinician review, and billing decision are not interchangeable events. The organization must verify qualified services, permissions, requirements, and staffing.

Is CarePlanLoop an operating product?

No. It is a research-stage concept with synthetic-only previews. There are no live integrations, customers, or product claims established.

Does the demo make clinical decisions?

No. It shows a reported event, provenance, an assigned review role, and unresolved or abstained states. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, or adjust a dose.

Does a device reading or automated touch prove billability?

No. A reading, an automated touch, staff documentation, and a billing determination are different things. CCM and RPM are distinct services; the organization must verify applicable requirements.

Are integrations available?

No live integrations are represented. Data access and device-feed rights are pilot discovery requirements.

What is the pilot price?

The $1,200 per team per month plus $3,000 onboarding figure is a hypothetical test price, not a current offer or accepted market price.

What needs to happen before a pilot?

The proposed readiness gate is 8 clinical/RCM interviews, a governed retrospective replay on an appropriately consented cohort, device-feed rights assessment, a jointly signed paid-pilot scope, and data-access/security and human-oversight approvals.
